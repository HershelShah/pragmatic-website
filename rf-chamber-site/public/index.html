FAQ

Common questions

Is this an accredited compliance lab?

No. This is a pre-compliance, R&D, and measurement facility. It is not accredited to ISO 17025 and is not A2LA-listed, and results here cannot be used for formal certification. It’s designed to help you find and fix problems before you pay for accredited lab time — so your certification visit is a formality, not a debugging session.

What should I bring to my session?

Your device under test, any control laptop/firmware tools, and device-specific fixtures or cables. Standard chamber equipment [TODO: confirm equipment list] is included with your rental. We’ll send a prep checklist when your booking is confirmed.

Can someone help me run my tests?

Yes. The chamber is self-service by default, but paid on-site engineering support is available — from an extra pair of hands during setup to help interpreting your measurements. Mention it in your inquiry.

Will my project stay confidential?

Yes. You rent the chamber exclusively for your time slot, and we’re happy to sign a mutual NDA before your visit if your project requires one.
