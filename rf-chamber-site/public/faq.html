Testing & accreditation

What’s the difference between pre-compliance and full compliance testing?

Full compliance testing is performed at an accredited (ISO 17025 / A2LA) laboratory following the exact standardized procedures regulators require (FCC, CE/RED, etc.). Those results go into your formal certification filing.

Pre-compliance testing uses similar measurement techniques in a lower-cost setting to find problems early: emissions peaks near limits, cable layout issues, shielding gaps, antenna detuning. Fixing those before your accredited lab visit saves expensive re-test cycles — a single failed certification attempt often costs more than an entire pre-compliance campaign.

This facility does pre-compliance and R&D measurement only. We are not accredited, and results from this chamber cannot be submitted for certification.

Can I use results from this chamber for FCC or CE certification?

No. Certification requires testing at an accredited laboratory. What this chamber gives you is confidence that you’ll pass when you get there — and hard data to debug with if your design isn’t ready yet.

What kinds of tests can I run?

Typical uses include pre-compliance radiated emissions scans, antenna measurements (comparisons between design revisions, integration effects), receiver desense hunting, EMI debugging on motors and power electronics, and general wireless R&D in an environment isolated from outside signals.
